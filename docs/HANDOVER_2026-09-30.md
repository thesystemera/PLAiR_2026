## Done today

**Voice engine: Chatterbox-Turbo replaces Orpheus.** Commits `905eac9`, `0e2e041`.

- **What was built:**
  - `tts_chatterbox/` has its own venv.
  - `batch_t3.py` is a batched CUDA-graph decoder: 8 sentences at once, and token-for-token identical to the stock model.
  - `batch_vocoder.py` is a batched S3Gen vocoder.
  - `engine.py` is one scheduler loop.
  - It keeps the same `/tts`, `/abort` and `/health` contract as Orpheus.
- **Measured on the P6000:**
  - First audio is ~0.4–0.5 s. The median over 645 live renders is 0.52 s.
  - Throughput is ~6x real time with 8 sentences at once. Orpheus managed ~1.1x.
  - No runaway takes.
- **Voices:**
  - Jess is Chatterbox's built-in voice.
  - Leo is `voices/leo.wav` and the station voice is `voices/station.wav`. Both were designed with Qwen3 VoiceDesign; they are not real people.
- **Caches:** the old Orpheus voice caches were purged. The files are backed up at `D:\tts_candidates\orpheus_clip_backup`.
- **Orpheus:** `tts_server/` stays in the repo but is no longer loaded.
- **Scratch material:** candidates, bake-off WAVs and test scripts are in `D:\tts_candidates`.

## Working with the owner (learned today)

- **Research first:** research properly before proposing or downloading anything. The owner judges audio by ear.
- **Audio terms:** use real audio terms (proximity effect, reverb, EQ, gain in dB). Say "cached lines", never "clips". Write in plain prose, not bare numbered lists.
- **Keywords:** no keyword or regex gates on listener text. The DJs and other LLM judges decide.
- **Paralanguage:** it stays phonetic, with tildes. Never swap it for engine tags.
- **Overlap:** a host never overlaps herself. Hosts overlap each other.
- **Evidence:** check logs after every test. Verify claims with evidence (logs, DB, git) before answering.
