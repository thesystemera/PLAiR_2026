# PLAiR app guide

What the hosts know about the app itself. One entry per `## ` heading: the `Asks:` line holds the ways listeners
put the question (it is what the search matches best), the rest is the answer, written to the listener. The hosts
get the entries closest to what was asked and answer in their own words. Phone and desktop: the panels Queue,
Catalog, Radio, Playing and Account sit side by side on a computer and are tabs along the bottom on a phone (the
User panel on a computer is the Account tab on a phone).

## What PLAiR is
Asks: what is this, what can this app do, what is PLAiR, how does this work, what am I listening to, what's this station, tell me about the app, what's the point of this
PLAiR is your own radio station with two live hosts, Jess and Leo, and a station computer for time checks and IDs. The music never stops, and you can talk to the hosts any time by voice or text: ask for a song, a style or a mood, skip or go back, or ask what's on near you, the weather, the news, gigs and places. It knows where you are (if you allow your location), so it can tell you what's happening around you, and listeners leave each other shoutouts, replies and reviews that go on air. You can search and browse the whole catalog yourself, start a station from any song, turn on Radio Mode for news and talk breaks between songs, download songs for offline, upload your own music and share tracks as videos.

## Talking to the hosts
Asks: how do I talk to you, can you hear me, how do I ask for a song, how do I speak to the DJs, can I talk to the radio, how do I use my voice
Press and hold the big round orb in the middle of the Radio panel, say what you want, and let go to send it; a spinner shows while it's being heard. On a computer you can also hold the Space bar or P to talk. Guests can talk to the hosts too.

## Typing to the hosts
Asks: can I type instead, can I text you, I don't want to talk out loud, how do I send a message, is there a chat
On a phone, tap the Radio tab again to switch it to Text; on a computer, use the round "Type to the DJs" button at the top of the Radio panel. Type in the "Message the DJs..." box and press Enter or the send button. Tap again to switch back to voice. The hosts can answer on air or with a private text that only you see in the chat.

## What the hosts can do for you
Asks: what can you do, what can I ask you, can you play something, can you skip this, what commands are there, can you change the music, what can the DJs do
Just ask in your own words. The hosts can find and play songs by name, artist, style, mood or lyrics, skip, go back, pause, restart or jump within a song, drop a song from the queue, start a station that sounds like the current song or mixes several things at once, play the charts, and switch Radio Mode or its talk breaks on and off. They can tell you the news, the weather, what gigs and events are on, places nearby, an artist's story, what a song's lyrics mean, and play listeners' shoutouts. With an account they can also like or ban songs for you, play your favorites, save your shoutouts and reviews, and move the music to your other device.

## Searching for music
Asks: how do I search, how do I find a song, how do I look for an artist, where's the search, can I search by voice
Use the "Search or speak..." bar at the top of the Catalog. Typing shows matching songs as you go and you tap one to play it; pressing Enter runs a smart search that understands what you mean ("rainy day jazz", "songs about leaving") and queues the best matches, playing the top one. Hold the small mic beside the bar to search by voice. The X clears the search.

## The catalog
Asks: where are all the songs, how do I browse, can I see every track, how many songs are there, where do I find genres
The Catalog panel shows every song with its count and total time. Sort it with "Recent", "A-Z" or "Genre"; Genre shows a card for each genre to browse. Tap any song to play it. New songs carry a "NEW" badge and songs already in your queue get a blue ring.

## Starting a station from a song
Asks: more like this, how do I find something like this, play similar songs, make a station from this song, how do I get songs like this one, radio from this track, seed radio
Tap "Seed radio" at the top of the Queue to build a station from the song that's playing, by "Main Genre", "Sub-Genres", "Mood", "More from Artist", "Similar Artists", "Style", "Theme", "Lyrics", "Vocal Style" or "All Categories". Each song card in the Catalog also has a list button to start a station from that song. Or just ask the hosts for "more like this", or a mix like "this style but slower and moodier".

## Charts and your own music
Asks: what's popular, top hits, the charts, play my favorites, play my liked songs, discovery, what are other people listening to
Tap "Charts" at the top of the Queue for "All-Time Hits", "This Week's Hits" and "Today's Hits", the songs PLAiR's listeners play most. "Your music" next to it plays "My Favorites" (your liked songs on shuffle, the ones you love most more often) or "Smart Discovery" (your favorites mixed with new songs close to them); those need an account.

## The queue
Asks: what's coming up next, where's the queue, how do I remove a song from the queue, what's after this song, can I change the order
The Queue panel shows the songs just played, the one on now and what's coming up. Tap any song to jump to it, or the X to take it out. When you or the hosts pick songs they go to the front; the station keeps filling the rest to match.

## Now Playing, lyrics and song details
Asks: where are the lyrics, what's this song about, who made this, show me the lyrics, song info, how popular is this song, what genre is this
The Playing panel shows the cover big, the lyrics in time with the song, and below them the song's genre, mood, theme, vocals, similar artists and audio details like energy and tempo, plus its plays, likes and skips. The buttons on the cover go fullscreen, show the reviews, share the song and make new songs from it. The lyrics also float through the visuals as they're sung. The hosts can explain what a song's lyrics mean if you ask.

## Likes, super likes and bans
Asks: how do I like a song, how do I save a song, I never want to hear this again, block this song, what's a super like, how do I favourite this
The heart likes a song, the star super likes it (one that really defines your taste), and the ban button means you never get it again. They're on every song card, on Now Playing and on shoutouts, and they shape what your station plays. You can also just tell the hosts "I love this" or "never play this again". You'll find them all in Account under "My Library". Likes need an account.

## Shoutouts
Asks: what are shoutouts, how do I give a shoutout, can I say hi to other listeners, how do I post something, can I leave a message for everyone
Shoutouts are short messages from listeners that the hosts play on air for others nearby. To post one, ask the hosts ("give a shoutout to...") by voice or text; an editor checks it's worth keeping first. Tap the Catalog tab again (or "View Shoutouts") to browse and play everyone's shoutouts. Posting needs an account. Other listeners only ever see your suburb, never where you are exactly.

## Replies
Asks: how do I reply to a shoutout, can I answer someone, respond to that message
Open a shoutout and tap the mic "Reply" button to record an answer, or type one in "Type a reply...". You can also tell the hosts you want to reply to the shoutout that just played. The best replies play on air right after their shoutout. Replies need an account.

## Reviews
Asks: how do I review a song, can I say what I think of this track, what did other people think of this song, leave a review
Tap "Reviews" on the Playing panel to read what listeners said about the song, and to write one ("What do you think of this song?") or record one with the mic. A good spoken review can play over the song for other listeners in a quiet moment. You can also tell the hosts your review out loud. Posting needs an account.

## Radio Mode and talk breaks
Asks: can you do news, I want more talking, I want less talking, turn off the talk breaks, what is Radio Mode, news on the hour, can you stop talking so much, too much talking
Radio Mode makes PLAiR run like a real station: news on the hour, a weather and city update at half past, and short features between songs (local gigs, community shoutouts, trivia, and now and then a story made just for you). A song always finishes before the hosts take over. It's on from the start. In the Account panel under "Radio Mode" one switch turns everything on or off: talk breaks, stings and time checks, and listener reviews over songs. Below it each one has its own switch, and you set how often features come. Or just ask the hosts to turn it on or off.

## Human or AI music
Asks: is this AI, is this music real, who made these songs, are these real artists, only human music, only AI music, why does it say inspired by
The catalog has AI-made songs, each written in the style of an artist and shown as "Inspired by" that artist, and songs that listeners made and uploaded themselves. The badge on the Playing panel says "100% Human" or "100% AI". In Account under Radio Mode, "Music" sets your station to Both, Human or AI; the hosts can switch it for you too.

## Location
Asks: how do you know where I am, why do you need my location, is my location private, who can see where I am, turn off location, what's near me
If you allow your location, the station knows what's around you: the local weather, gigs, places to go and what other listeners nearby are saying. Without it the hosts can only guess from your timezone. Your exact position is used only by your own station; other listeners only ever see your suburb or a rough distance, never your address. You can say no and it won't ask again; your browser's site settings change it later.

## Timeline: what played
Asks: what was that song earlier, what did you just say, what played before, show me what's been on, I missed that, history
The clock button at the top of the Radio panel opens your Timeline: everything that aired for you in the last 24 hours, newest first: songs, shoutouts, replies, reviews, news and talk, with filters. Tap a talk entry to read what was said. You can also ask the hosts about something that played earlier.

## Playing on another device
Asks: play it on my phone, move the music to my computer, switch device, listen on another device, it's playing on the wrong device
With an account the music follows you: open PLAiR on another device and it takes over playback (you can turn that off with "Auto-switch playback to the device I open"). The devices button on the player lists your devices; tap one to move the music there. On the other device a "Playing on another device" notice has a "Play here" button. You can also ask the hosts to move it. This needs an account.

## Offline and downloads
Asks: can I listen offline, does it work without internet, download songs, save for offline, no signal, on the plane
Every song you play to the end is kept on your device, and with an account your liked and super liked songs download in the background (up to 500 MB a day). Without a connection PLAiR keeps playing from your downloads, the Catalog shows only those, and your likes sync when you're back. The hosts can't hear you while you're offline. Manage the space in Account under "Storage & Performance"; "Data Saver Mode" keeps streaming light.

## Uploading your music
Asks: can I upload my own songs, how do I put my music on here, I'm a musician, add my track, upload a video
Tap the green "Upload your music" button in the Catalog, or go to Account and "My Uploads". Drop in an audio file (MP3, WAV, FLAC and more, up to 100 MB) or a video (we take the sound from it), choose which of your artists it's by, and you can close the window: it tells you when it's live. PLAiR works out the title, genre, mood, lyrics and cover, and masters the sound to station level, optionally enhancing it; you can edit all of it afterwards, choose Public, Unlisted or Private, and delete it any time. Your songs then play on the station for other listeners. Uploading needs an account.

## Artists and bands
Asks: how do I add my band, change my artist name, artist profile, credit my songs to my band
In Account under "Artists & Bands" you add the names you release under, with an optional bio and links. Uploads are credited to the artist you choose (your username until you add one), and renaming an artist updates all its songs. Listeners see your bio under "About" on the Playing panel.

## Making songs
Asks: can I make my own song, generate music, create a song, make a remix, make a song like this, can you make one
With an account you can have PLAiR make new songs: just ask the hosts ("make us a Michael Jackson style track"), type an idea in the Catalog search bar and tap the purple sparkles "Generate tracks", or tap "+" on the Playing panel to remix the current song or make more like its artist or similar artists. Each uses credits; free accounts get a few a month and Premium gets more. New songs go into your queue when they're ready, usually ten minutes or so, and join the catalog for everyone.

## Sharing a song
Asks: how do I share this, send this song to a friend, make a video of this song, share link
Tap the share button on the Playing panel. You can copy or share a link to the song, or "Generate Video" to make a vertical music video with the song's visuals and lyrics, then download or share it.

## Visuals and smoothness
Asks: the visuals are lagging, it's slow on my phone, how do I make it smoother, turn off the moving covers, fullscreen visuals, battery
The visuals react to the music, with covers that move in 3D as you tilt your phone. If it feels slow, open Account and "Storage & Performance": set "Visual Quality" to LOW or AUTO, keep "Keep It Smooth" on, or turn off "3D Lit Artwork". Tap the cover on the player (or press F) for fullscreen visuals.

## Sound, voices and audio quality
Asks: mute the DJs, turn off the hosts' voices, turn off the pings, audio quality, better sound, the music is too quiet
The sound button beside the player's cover cycles "DJ + PING" (hosts and notification sounds), "DJ only", "PING only" and "OFF". The quality button next to it sets "Auto", "Economy" (128k), "Standard" (192k) or "Premium" (256k); guests stay on Auto up to 192k, and 256k needs an account. Account under "Audio & Devices" picks your microphone and speakers.

## Controls, gestures and shortcuts
Asks: how do I skip, how do I pause, swipe controls, keyboard shortcuts, go back a song
The player along the bottom has previous (restart once a song is three seconds in), play/pause and next, and you can tap the waveform to jump. On the Radio orb, swipe up to play, down to pause, left for the next song and right for the previous one. On a computer: K play/pause, J previous, L next, M mute, the arrow keys jump 5 seconds, F fullscreen, and hold Space or P to talk. Or just ask the hosts.

## Making an account
Asks: how do I sign up, should I make an account, why sign in, create an account, register, what do I get with an account, is it free, do I need an email
An account is free and makes the station yours: likes, super likes and bans that shape what plays, your favorites and Smart Discovery stations, posting shoutouts, replies and reviews, your station and settings on all your devices with the music following you between them, liked songs downloaded for offline, uploading your own music, making songs, and the best audio quality. It takes a few seconds and needs no email and no password: in the Account panel (the Account tab on a phone) tap "Create account", pick a username and tap "Create with passkey"; Windows Hello, Face ID or a fingerprint signs you in. A password is optional.

## Signing in on another device
Asks: how do I log in on my computer, sign in on another phone, I can't log in, log in, scan code, QR code
In the Account panel tap "Login" and then "Sign in with passkey" (Windows Hello, Face ID, a fingerprint or your phone). On a new device you can also choose "Scan from a signed-in phone": it shows a QR code and a short code; scan it with your signed-in phone's camera, or type the code in Account under "Account & Privacy" on that phone, and confirm "Sign it in". "Use a password" works if you set one.

## Your account and privacy
Asks: how do I log out, delete my account, delete my data, change my username, profile picture, clear the conversation, privacy
In the Account panel tap your picture to add a photo and the pencil to change your username. "Account & Privacy" adds passkeys, sets an optional password, clears your conversation with the hosts, resets the profile the hosts keep of your taste, and "Delete account and all data" removes everything for good, ending any subscription. "Logout" is at the bottom.

## Premium
Asks: what's premium, how much does it cost, is it free, subscription, upgrade, pay
PLAiR is free to listen to, talk to and sign up for. Premium is a monthly subscription that gives you many more credits for making songs and supports the station; "Upgrade to Premium" in the Account panel shows the price, and "Manage subscription" changes or cancels it.
